We are pleased to announce that the following two papers have been accepted at [WACV 2024](https://wacv2024.thecvf.com/):

* [Self-Supervised Representation Learning with Cross-Context Learning between Global and Hypercolumn Features](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.13392.pdf), Zheng Gao, Chen Feng, and Ioannis Patras.
* [Improving Fairness using Vision-Language Driven Image Augmentation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.01573.pdf), Moreno D’Incà, Christos Tzelepis, Ioannis Patras, and Nicu Sebe.
