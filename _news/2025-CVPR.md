* Anxhelo Diko, Tinghuai Wang, Wassim Swaileh, Shiyan Sun, Ioannis Patras, **ReWind: Understanding Long Videos with Instructed Learnable Memory**, CVPR 2025.
* Yu Cao, Zengqun Zhao, Ioannis Patras, Shaogang Gong, **Temporal Score Analysis for Understanding and Correcting Diffusion Artifacts**, CVPR 2025.
