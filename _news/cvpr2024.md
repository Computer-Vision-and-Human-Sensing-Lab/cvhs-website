We are pleased to announce that the following three papers have been accepted at [CVPR 2024](https://cvpr2024.thecvf.com/):


* [Zhonglin Sun](https://github.com/szlbiubiubiu), [Chen Feng](https://mrchenfeng.github.io/), [Ioannis Patras](http://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~ioannisp/), and [Georgios Tzimiropoulos](https://ytzimiro.github.io/), **["LAFS: Landmark-based Facial Self-supervised Learning for Face Recognition"](https://github.com/szlbiubiubiu/LAFS_CVPR2024)**, CVPR 2024.
* [Zheng Gao](https://zaczgao.github.io/) and [Ioannis Patras](http://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~ioannisp/), **["Self-Supervised Facial Representation Learning with Facial Region Awareness"](https://github.com/zaczgao/Facial_Region_Awareness)**, CVPR 2024.
